Local Population Studies Society

Dedicated to the study of local history, social history, and historical demography.

The Society closed in September 2026 after a final conference at Madingley Hall, Cambridge, where the journal Local Population Studies was first conceived in the summer of 1968.

About the Society

The Local Population Studies Society (LPSS) was founded in 1972 as a charity devoted to promoting local history, social history, and historical demography in a local context.

The Committee would like to thank its members, authors, editors, reviewers, conference speakers, and supporters for their contributions over more than fifty years.

Legacy and Archives

  • The Society's remaining funds have been converted to a fund for research in historical demography, administered by the British Society for Population Studies. Further details are available at: BSPS Awards and Grants .
  • A copy of the original LPSS website has been preserved by the Internet Archive.
  • The Local Population Studies Journal is archived in two locations:
    • Journal archive on Archive.org
    • Journal archive on Zenodo (as a single zip download)
  • The 404 Parish Register Dataset is available via the Internet Archive:
    • Detailed list of parishes
