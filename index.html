Society Closure Notice
The Local Population Studies Society (LPSS) ceased operations in 2026 following its final conference. The Society had promoted local history, social history and historical demography since 1972.

The Committee would like to thank its members, authors, editors, reviewers, conference speakers, and supporters for their contributions over more than fifty years.

To support ongoing research, the Society's journal, Local Population Studies, remains freely available online. The archive contains articles and research covering local history, historical demography, family history, population studies and related fields.

About the Society

Founded in 1972, the Local Population Studies Society was a charitable organisation devoted to promoting the study of population, family, community and social history in local contexts. Alongside publication of its journal, the Society organised conferences, supported research, and encouraged collaboration between professional historians, independent researchers and family historians. The Society closed in September 2026.
