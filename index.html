Important notice: The Local Population Studies Society (LPSS) is scheduled to be wound up following its final conference in 2026. This website is retained to preserve access to the Society's publications and historical resources.

About the Society

Founded in 1972, the Local Population Studies Society promoted local history, social history, and historical demography. The Society published the journal Local Population Studies, organised conferences, supported historical research, and encouraged the study of populations in the past.

Although the Society is no longer active, its publications and resources remain available for researchers, students, family historians, and anyone interested in local and population history.
